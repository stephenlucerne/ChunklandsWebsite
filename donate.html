How automatic ranks work

Payment platforms don't know your Minecraft username, so we link the two with a one-time code you generate in game. The server recognises the code, the payment provider confirms the payment, and a rank lands on your account. Three steps, about thirty seconds.

  1. Get your code in game

    Join the server and run /donate. You'll get a short personal code, for example CL-4F9K2Q, tied to your account.

  2. Enter it here and choose an amount

    Fill in the form on this page. Your code travels with the payment, so the system knows exactly who the donation is for.

  3. Pay, and the rank applies itself

    Your rank is granted automatically, usually within a few seconds and at worst before your next server restart. Join and check with /rank.

Prefer not to create a code? You can still donate directly below and message staff with your username. We'll grant the rank manually.

Donation questions

Before you donate

Will you really auto-deliver the rank?
Yes. Once the automatic flow is enabled, the rank is granted from a confirmed payment, not from a form submission. Until then, staff apply it manually.
What if my rank doesn't arrive?
Message staff in Discord with your username and the code you used. Payments are verified against the provider before anything is granted, so anything paid for gets delivered.
Does my username matter?
Yes. The rank is granted to a Minecraft account, so your username must match the name you connect with. If you play on cracked clients and your name changes, tell staff and we'll move it.
Is it one-time or recurring?
One-time. Ranks don't expire and there are no subscriptions. Some ranks include a recurring token top-up, which is noted on the ranks page.
Can I get a rank without spending money?
Yes. Vote for the server, take on daily quests, and play long enough. In-game ranks are earnable and that's the point of a beta. Donations are a shortcut, not the only route.
Is it really OK to donate while the server is in beta?
Yes. Hosting costs don't wait for the beta to finish, and supporting the server now is what keeps the beta running so you can play it. Ranks are cosmetic and perk-based. Nothing that makes you win.
