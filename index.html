The core loop

Start small.
Stay a long time.

Most servers either hand you a big map or lock you into a floating island. Chunk Lands does neither. You get one chunk and a reason to keep logging in.

  1. 1 Spawn on a single 16×16 chunk 256 blocks of dirt, grass and whatever's underneath it. It's all yours, including the bad parts.
  2. 2 Play normally, earn tokens Mine, build, farm, fight. Everything you do fills up the token economy. Skills count too. This is a full McMMO server.
  3. 3 Spend tokens on adjacent chunks Each neighbouring chunk is purchasable. Price climbs the further you push out, so early land is cheap and the frontier is expensive.
  4. 4 Uncover a world-sized plot Buy enough chunks and you've got a substantially large piece of land with a real build on it. That's the whole game.

It's Skyblock's endgame without Skyblock's restrictions. Full survival progression, mob spawning, redstone and nether access all still in play.

Your land over time

Chunk costs climb the further you push from spawn, so the first few buys are cheap and the frontier gets expensive. Exact pricing is shown in-game.

Questions

FAQ

How do I join?
Add play.chunklands.net to your Minecraft server list and connect. No whitelist or application needed right now. You're free to play immediately.
What do I actually start with?
One 16×16 chunk: 256 blocks. Nothing else, no items, no gear. Everything after that you earn or buy yourself. It is deliberately a hard start.
Is this just Skyblock?
The land expansion is the same idea, but nothing else is restricted. You play on a normal overworld with normal survival rules, mob spawning and full item access. Check the comparison for the specifics.
How do I buy more land?
Earn tokens through normal play, quests and skill progression, then spend them on chunks adjacent to land you already own. Prices rise the further out you go.
Is there a whitelist?
Not at the moment. The point of the beta is to get as many new players through the door as possible. Join, play a bit, and let us know what you think.
Which version and edition?
Our Discord is coming soon. Until then, join the server and ask in chat and someone will help you out.
How do ranks work?
Ranks are available through donations and are applied automatically to your account. No staff request needed. See Ranks for what each one includes.
Can I donate and get a rank automatically?
Yes. Run /donate in game to get your personal redeem code, enter it on the donate page, pay, and the rank lands on your account automatically. If something goes wrong, message staff and it'll be sorted by hand.
Something's broken / I found a bug
Tell us in Discord with what you were doing and roughly when it happened. Bug reports from beta players are the single most useful thing you can send us.
I want to build the spawn area
Yes please. Drop your portfolio or a link to your builds in Discord with the builder tag. Read more.
